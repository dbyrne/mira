# Esprit 120 — Emission Nebula Image Book (single-frame)

Single-frame framing previews for **emission nebulae** (HII / planetary / SNR / WR) that fit one Esprit 120 frame (**1.60°×1.07°**, 96'×64' @ 840mm, ASI2600MM) and are observable from Jersey City this **summer/fall**. Mosaics excluded by design — these are the objects that work as a single shot. Coordinates resolved via SIMBAD; yellow box = Esprit FOV at the suggested rotation; DSS2 color (Ha reads brown here — through your 3nm filters it's vivid red/SHO). Sorted by peak month.

**Excluded as too low from JC (<35° / deep southern Milky Way):** Lagoon M8, Trifid M20, Eagle M16, Swan M17. **Excluded as too big for one frame (mosaic/wide-field):** North America NGC7000, Heart & Soul IC1805/1848, California NGC1499, full IC1396, Sh2-119 Clamshell.
